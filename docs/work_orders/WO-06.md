PART A — THE WIND FIELD (src/swm/stages/storm.py; spec §4.3b)

A1 Stage order: terrain → layers → requirements → couplings → buffers, strength, repair → storm → scenarios. The storm stage reads terrain and the recipe only. It draws from new streams shared_causes/storm_corridor/<scenario index>/track and .../variation; retire the unused shared_causes/storm_corridor/field stream if nothing draws it, as PR 5b did.

A2 For each scenario, draw a straight track across the island (position and angle) and a peak 10-minute wind at sea within the severity's CWA class band. Build the field on the terrain grid in the §4.3b order:
  1. modified Rankine profile, exponent 0.5 (sqrt only);
  2. sea to land 0.83, the drop mostly within 1 km of the coast;
  3. Taiwan wind-code surface factor by land cover: city core 0.476, town 0.662, open or coast 1.0;
  4. mountain lee 0.6 on the lee side of the spine relative to the track;
  5. random variation from a Wendland kernel (1 − t)⁴(1 + 4t), correlation length from the recipe, spread 10%.
  Winds are stored in m/s at 10-minute averaging. Gust conversion (×1.75 inland, ×1.30 coast) happens only in the line fragility (PR 6b).

A3 Radius of maximum wind: the evidence gives a geometric mean of 47.5 km (Willoughby & Rahn 2004), larger than the pilot island. Put it in the registry as a placeholder and record in the report how much of the pilot lies inside it.

A4 Normal values for the random variation are built from uniform draws with permitted arithmetic only (no Box–Muller: it needs log and cos). A Cholesky or kernel-sum construction is your choice; mark it CHOICE.

PART B — SEVERITY AND FLOOD (spec §4.3b)

B1 Severity: V_max at sea = 17.2 + 0.53 × severity m/s; severity 0 means no storm. Rainfall 300 mm up to severity 33, 450 mm up to 66, 600 mm above. Replace the schema description "100 = every exposed element in the hazard core fails" with the new meaning. Storm correlation_km default becomes 10 (the random-variation correlation length). Pilot recipe: storm severity 50. Recipe version 2 is unreleased (no tag yet), so no version bump; say so in the report.

B2 Flood depth at each grid cell = the scenario's flood stage (1, 2 or 3 m by rainfall) − the cell's height above the nearest drainage (from the terrain's drainage network). Record per facility: height above drainage, flood depth per scenario.

B3 Every §4.3b hazard number goes in the rules registry with its source, grade, reasoning and status, and a Theta role field `theta_role: dynamics` (the Theta List format is draft v0.1; add only this field now).

PART D — FRAGILITY AND DAMAGE (src/swm/stages/scenarios.py)

D1 Overhead lines (power links, power feeds from a substation to its consumers, telecom links): failure chance per km from a stored table indexed by 3-second gust, centred on 0.05 per km at 45 m/s; build the table offline (tools/), stored in the registry. A line reads its peak gust along its length. Where a power feed has no drawn geometry, use the straight distance and mark CHOICE. Transmission towers: the Fani curve (collapse median about 285 km/h) as a stored table; they should not fail at pilot severity.

D2 Facilities fail by flood only, at their Hazus functional depths (§4.3b list). Damage state by depth over the threshold: up to 0.5 m slight, above 0.5 m moderate, above 1.5 m extensive, above 3 m complete. A recipe control caps facility damage (pilot: moderate).

D3 Given the field, each element fails independently from its own stream (conditional independence). Record per scenario: every damaged element, its damage state, and the field value that drove it.

PART E — REPAIR OF LINES

E1 A failed overhead line takes 6 h crew work, no lead time, plus road travel from the crew base (existing travel term). Add it to the repair records, so the restorative model sequences crews across failed nodes and failed lines.

PART F — WHAT THE GENERATOR DOES NOT DO

F1 Protection (the defensive model's "protect condition") is applied by the solver, not the generator: the generator records each element's damage per scenario, and a protected element is read as undamaged. Record, per element, whether it is protectable (every facility and every overhead line).

F2 Dynamics (condition 0 until repaired, cascades, buffers) is Work Order 7.

PART G — CHECKS

G1 World check: at severity 0, no element is damaged in any scenario.
G2 Report per scenario: damaged facilities by class and state, failed lines, and total km of line down. For the pilot, report whether each of the 8 scenarios damages at least one element; if none does, report it and do not tune.

TESTS (across both PRs)
- Identity (golden) for the storm field and the scenario set on the pilot, on both platforms.
- Field: the profile is monotone outside R_max; each §4.3b factor applies where it should (sea vs land, city vs open, lee vs windward); variation has the stated correlation length and spread within tolerance.
- Severity: V_max at the class boundaries; severity 0 gives no wind and no damage.
- Flood: depth = stage − height above drainage; each class's threshold at the boundary.
- Fragility: the stored tables reproduce the offline curves at their knots; lines fail more often at higher gust (property test).
- Scenarios: changing the scenario count does not move earlier scenarios' draws; changing the error seed moves nothing here.
- Separation: no hazard field value reaches an emitted source file (it is unobserved).
