title: Two supplies into one node
values: 
rule: Two feeds of one supply add up, each giving κ × its supplier's state, to at most 1. Within its own network, a node takes the best of its incoming links.
illustration: A -> C power 0.5 ; B -> C power 0.5
drill: C takes power from A and from B, κ = 0.5 each, and throttles. A is damaged. What is C's state at hour 0?
answer: 0.5: B's half arrives; A's does not.
