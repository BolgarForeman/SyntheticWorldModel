title: Weakest supply wins
values: failure_modes.combining
rule: A node works only as well as its weakest supply. Its state is the lowest of its responses to everything it needs ({failure_modes.combining}).
illustration: A -> C power 0.6 ; B -> C water 0.8
drill: C takes power from A at κ = 0.6 and water from B at κ = 0.8, and throttles on both. A and B are working. What is C's state at hour 0?
answer: 0.6: the lower of 0.6 and 0.8, not their product.
expect: 0.6
